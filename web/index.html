The picture

—

Frame rate is the biggest thing a controller feels: at 30 every press waits up to 33ms for the next frame, at 60 half that.

4000 kb/s

There is no congestion control here, so a figure the link cannot carry does not soften the picture — it queues packets, which is delay. If it stutters, come down rather than up.

60 ms

How long your browser holds the picture before drawing it, to absorb packets that arrive late. Straight delay: on a cable 40–60 is plenty, on mobile data 100 or more stops it juddering.

The ones nobody should need
60 ms

How much encoded video may pile up for a guest whose link is struggling before frames are dropped instead.

100 ms

Small stops the encoder smoothing bursts by holding frames back, which is exactly what adds delay.

Applying rebuilds the stream: everybody watching loses about a second of picture.
